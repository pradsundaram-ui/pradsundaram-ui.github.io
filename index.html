About me

Starting my career in Australia in 2016 at Rotor Studios, Animal Logic, Iloura, and Luma Pictures, I developed a versatile generalist toolkit. I spent three and a half years at ILM Sydney before relocating to ILM London to continue pushing my creative boundaries.

My work includes key contributions to major IPs across DC, Disney, and Marvel Studios, including Aquaman, Wicked, Spider-Man, Thor, and Avengers.

At my core, I am a problem solver who thrives on continuous growth and artistic excellence. My ultimate goal on any team is simple: to be the reliable, versatile go-to artist who gets the job done seamlessly, no matter the scale or complexity.
